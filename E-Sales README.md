# 🛍️ Online Retail Sales Analysis

## 📌 About

This project explores online retail transaction data to understand sales
performance, product demand, customer contribution, and purchasing
patterns.

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains online retail transactions with details such as
invoice number, product code, product description, quantity, invoice
date, unit price, customer ID, and country.

## 🧰 Tools

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Jupyter Notebook.

## 🔍 Analysis

-   Checked missing values, duplicate records, and data types.
-   Analysed products generating the highest revenue and products with
    the highest quantity sold.
-   Compared revenue contribution across countries.
-   Explored monthly sales trends and sales activity across weekdays and
    hours.
-   Calculated average order value and identified valuable customers.
-   Examined cancelled invoices and unusual transaction values.
-   Studied product demand patterns and the relationship between unit
    price and quantity purchased.

## 📊 Findings

-   The **United Kingdom** contributed the highest revenue among the
    countries in the dataset.
-   **DOTCOM POSTAGE** was the highest revenue-generating entry, while
    **Paper Craft, Little Birdie** had the highest total quantity sold.
-   Monthly revenue reached its highest level in **November 2011**.
    December 2011 is incomplete because the dataset ends on December 9.
-   The average order value was higher than the median order value,
    indicating a right-skewed order-value distribution.
-   A relatively small group of identified customers contributed
    substantially to total revenue.
-   The quantity and unit-price distributions contained extreme values
    that may need transaction-level review.
-   Approximately **14.81% of unique invoices** were cancelled.
-   Unit price and quantity purchased showed almost no linear
    correlation in this dataset.
-   Quantity and sales showed a strong positive correlation, as sales
    were calculated from quantity multiplied by unit price.
-   Thursday had the highest number of invoices among the weekdays
    represented.

## 💡 Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of product performance, customer
contribution, geographical revenue distribution, and sales trends.
Revenue was concentrated in the United Kingdom and among a smaller group
of products and customers. The analysis also highlighted differences
between high-revenue and high-quantity products, fluctuations in monthly
sales, cancelled invoices, and unusual transaction values. These
findings help describe purchasing patterns and identify areas for
further investigation. The results are descriptive and do not establish
causal relationships.
